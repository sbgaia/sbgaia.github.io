I am a Research Fellow in Computer Science at the University of Verona, where my
research focuses on the development of advanced methodologies for the
optimization, reconfiguration, and verification of Cyber-Physical Production 
Systems (CPPSs). 

I obtained my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Verona in 2025.
My doctoral research, titled "Dynamo: A Framework to Verify, Optimize and 
Reconfigure Flexible Manufacturing Systems", was conducted under the supervision 
of Prof. Franco Fummi.

Previously, I obtained my Master's degree (M.Sc.) in Computer Science and
Engineering (curriculum Embedded Systems) at the University of Verona in 2021 and
my Bachelor's degree (B.Sc.) in Computer Science at the University of Verona in 2019.

In addition to my academic work, I am a co-founder and scientific advisor for 
<a href="https://corvina.io/factoryal">FACTORYAL S.r.l.</a>, a startup specializing 
in factory automation software that originated as a spin-off from the University 
of Verona.
