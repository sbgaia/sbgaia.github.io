I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Verona, working on 
the development of new methodologies for the optimization, reconfiguration, and 
verification of Cyber-Physical Production Systems (CPPSs). 

I obtained my Master's degree (M.Sc.) in Computer Science and Engineering (curriculum 
Embedded Systems) at the University of Verona in 2021 with a thesis titled 
"Meta-MES: A Scheduling Architecture for Flexible Automation" under the supervision 
of Prof. Franco Fummi.

Previously, I obtained my Bachelor's degree (B.Sc.) in Computer Science at the 
University of Verona in 2019.

I am currently involved as a co-founder and scientific advisor for <a href="https://corvina.io/factoryal">FACTORYAL 
S.r.l.</a>, a startup specializing in factory automation software that originated as 
a spin-off from the University of Verona.
